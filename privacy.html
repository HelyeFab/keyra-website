Privacy Policy

Welcome to Keyra!

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information and outlines your rights as a user of Keyra.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to enhance your experience and ensure the app functions smoothly:

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and subscription preferences.
  • Usage Data: Reading progress, saved words, study statistics, and language preferences.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, and app version.
  • Interaction Data: How you interact with the app (e.g., feature usage and engagement).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used responsibly to improve your experience and our services:

  • Personalised Experience: Tailoring content, reading recommendations, and study features to your needs.
  • Service Improvements: Enhancing app performance, fixing issues, and adding new features.
  • Communication: Sending important updates, such as feature releases or account notifications.
  • Analytics: Aggregated and anonymised data to understand usage trends and improve the app.

3. Data Protection

We prioritise the security of your data and implement industry-standard practices:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data is encrypted during transmission and storage.
  • Access Controls: Limited access to authorised personnel only.
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews to identify and address potential vulnerabilities.

4. Your Rights

We respect your rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access Your Data: View the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct Information: Request corrections to inaccurate or outdated details.
  • Delete Your Data: Delete your account and associated data permanently.
  • Manage Preferences: Opt out of non-essential communications and notifications.
  • Export Your Data: Request a copy of your data in a portable format.

To exercise any of these rights, visit the Privacy Settings section within the app.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated above. Once no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

6. Cookies and Tracking

Keyra may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance your experience. These help us:

  • Save your language and content preferences.
  • Track app performance and troubleshoot issues.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your device settings.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We’ll notify you of significant updates through the app or email.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or feedback about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us directly:

  • In-App Support: Use the “Contact Us” feature within the app.
  • Email: privacy@keyraapp.com
